# Notification History

A personal history of notifications received by a dedicated Pushover device.

## Language

**Received notification**:
A notification delivered to this client's Pushover device, regardless of which application sent it. Notifications addressed exclusively to other devices are outside this history.

**Source application**:
The Pushover application that sent a notification. Sources appear through received notifications without manual registration.

**Read status**:
Whether the user has marked a notification as read in this client. This status is independent of other devices and of emergency acknowledgement.

**Emergency acknowledgement**:
An explicit acknowledgement of a Pushover emergency notification that stops its repeated alerts. Reading a notification in this client does not constitute emergency acknowledgement.

**Inbox**:
The collection of newly received notifications and notifications restored from Archived.
_Avoid_: Normal

**Archived notification**:
A retained notification moved out of Inbox. It can be restored and retains its read status.

**Permanent deletion**:
Removal of a notification and its attachments from the client's active history, with no Trash or restore operation. Reconnecting does not bring the notification back.

**Delete all history**:
A confirmed bulk action that permanently deletes notifications from both Inbox and Archived.
